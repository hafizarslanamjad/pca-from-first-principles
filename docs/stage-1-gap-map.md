# Stage 1: reading order

Lecture 4 follows the learner-selected text from the original Part 4: sections 1–5 and 7–9. Original section numbers are preserved. Section 6 on normalization is intentionally omitted from this selection.

The lecture moves from points and displacement to computational representations, addition, scaling, basis directions, coordinate descriptions, and feature-space differences.

The questions about numerical representations of direction, the meaning of magnitude, mixed units, normalization, and scaling unit vectors remain open for subsequent learner-guided material. This package does not treat those questions as resolved or advance to Stage 2.
